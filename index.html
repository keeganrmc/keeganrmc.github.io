About

I am an industrial designer based in Grand Rapids, Michigan, with an engineering background from Michigan State University.

Right now, I work as a technical illustrator at Haworth, creating product guides and technical illustrations directly from 3D CAD models.

I like building hands-on mockups in the shop with cardboard, foam, and 3D prints to test ideas physically before getting deep into CAD detailing.
