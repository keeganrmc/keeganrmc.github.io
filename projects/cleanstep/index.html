PROJECT 01•Pet Care•2025

CleanStep Litter Box

A modern litter box made with stainless steel that stops odors and traps stray litter.

Plastic litter boxes scratch easily and hold onto bad odors no matter how much you wash them. CleanStep pairs a non-porous stainless steel pan with an exit ramp that gently cleans cat paws as they step out, plus built-in side storage for scoops and waste bags.

View Full Resolution
The Problem

Why Current Options Fall Short

Plastic pans scratch over time, hold onto bad smells, and let cats kick litter all over the floor.

The Goal

A durable stainless steel pan that won’t absorb smells, an exit ramp that catches scattered litter, and side tool storage.

User Feedback3 INTERVIEWS
Betsy (2 Cats)

“I want a box with plenty of room inside that doesn’t take over the whole room, and no motorized parts that break.”

Takeaway:Roomy interior without bulky size or electronic parts that can jam.
Stella (Apartment)

“My litter box has to fit in a small bathroom. I need to be able to scoop fast and keep bags and scoops handy.”

Takeaway:Compact shape with built-in storage for bags and the scoop.
Patti & Tom (Older Cat)

“High sides keep litter in, but our older cat has trouble hopping over tall walls.”

Takeaway:A gentle entry step makes it easy to walk in while catching tracked litter.
Details

Key Features

Won’t Hold Odors

Stainless Steel Pan

Smooth stainless steel wipes clean easily and won’t absorb odors like plastic does.

Catches Stray Litter

Paw-Cleaning Ramp

A textured ramp catches loose litter off paws and drops it right back into the box.

Everything in One Spot

Tool Storage Caddy

Magnetic dock holds the scoop and trash bags neatly on the side so they stay off the floor.

Fast Daily Scooping

Quick-Lift Hood

The top lifts off with one hand for quick scooping, and has a curved corner for easy dumping.
