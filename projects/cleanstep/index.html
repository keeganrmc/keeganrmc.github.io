PROJECT 01•Pet Hardware • User Experience•2025

CleanStep Cat Care System

Furniture-grade cat care featuring a non-porous stainless steel basin, paw-cleaning ramp, and integrated tool dock.

Plastic litter pans scratch and trap bacteria and ammonia odors that cannot be washed out. CleanStep pairs a non-porous 304 stainless steel basin with an integrated paw-cleaning exit step and modular supply dock, turning daily maintenance into a clean, quiet routine.

View Full Resolution
The Problem

User Need & Friction

Porous plastic pans scratch easily, trap persistent ammonia odors, and allow cats to scatter litter across living floors.

Design Objective

A sanitary 304 stainless steel basin that never absorbs odor, an exit ramp that traps stray granules, and a magnetic tool dock.

User Fieldwork & Insights3 INTERVIEWS
Betsy (2 Cats)

“I want a spacious box without exterior bulk. No motorized parts that jam.”

Takeaway:Spacious interior volume without motorized failure points.
Stella (Urban Apartment)

“It has to fit a tight bathroom. I need quick scooping and built-in tool storage.”

Takeaway:Compact footprint with integrated tool storage.
Patti & Tom (Senior Cat)

“Older cats cannot jump tall edges. We need easy low-incline entry.”

Takeaway:Low-incline perforated entry step catches stray litter.

Key Features & Detailing

Zero Odor Absorption

304 Stainless Steel Basin

Non-porous stainless steel wipes clean effortlessly and never absorbs ammonia odor.

Stops Floor Tracking

Paw-Cleaning Catch Ramp

Perforated ramp gently spreads paw pads, dropping loose granules back into the pan.

Integrated Tool Dock

Modular Supply Caddy

Magnetic exterior dock keeps scoop and waste bags organized and off clean floors.

Effortless Maintenance

Quick-Release Hood & Chute

Single-motion lift-off hood with a rear pour-out corner for mess-free litter changes.
