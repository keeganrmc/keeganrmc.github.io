CleanStep Litter Box

A modern litter box made with stainless steel that stops odors and traps stray litter.

RoleIndustrial Design, Physical Mockups, CAD
Duration15 Weeks
ScopeSolo Design Project
Tools & MethodsRhino3D, KeyShot, Cardboard Mockups, 3D Printing
01 / Background & Goals

The Design Challenge

Traditional plastic litter boxes scratch, harbor bacteria, and permanently hold onto odors. CleanStep pairs a non-porous stainless steel basin with an incline paw-cleaning ramp to stop tracking and keep floors clean.
