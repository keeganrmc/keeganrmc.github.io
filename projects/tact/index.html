TACT Ergonomic Input Tool

An ergonomic CAD mouse shaped through 14 hand-carved foam mockups to eliminate repetitive wrist strain.

TACT rotates the hand from flat desk pronation into an anatomically neutral 52° handshake angle, reducing forearm tendon tension while preserving fingertip control for micro-precision CAD modeling.

The Problem

User Need & Friction

Vertical mice feel clumsy for fine CAD modeling, while traditional flat mice cause repetitive wrist strain.

Design Objective

A sculpted 52° handshake volume with a flared thumb shelf for all-day precision modeling.

Ergonomic Validation

TACT rotates the hand from flat desk pronation into an anatomically neutral 52° handshake angle, reducing forearm tendon tension while preserving fingertip control for micro-precision CAD modeling.

Physical scale validation•Hands-on testing
Architecture

Key Features & Detailing

Anatomical Balance

52° Neutral Handshake Angle

Eliminates forearm pronation while keeping fingertips close to the desk for steady cursor tracking.

Eliminates Desk Drag

Contoured Thumb Flare

Cradles the thenar muscle group, preventing thumb friction and desk drag.

Tactile Stepping

Magnetic Stepped Scroll Wheel

Machined aluminum flywheel with switchable tactile detents for precise CAD zoom.
