ALINE Trail Chair

A lightweight camp chair that turns your trekking poles into back support.

RoleIndustrial Design, Functional Prototyping, CMF
Duration15 Weeks
ScopeSolo Design Project
Tools & MethodsPhysical Prototyping, Pattern Making, Field Testing, SolidWorks
01 / Background & Goals

The Design Challenge

Sitting on rocks or wet logs after hiking 15 miles hurts your back, but traditional camp chairs weigh 2 pounds. ALINE uses the hiking poles you already carry to create a comfortable, reclining trail chair that weighs under 10 ounces.
